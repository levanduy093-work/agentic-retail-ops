# Warranty coverage for NovaSound Mini

NovaSound Mini has a 12-month limited warranty from the delivery date. The
warranty covers manufacturing defects in the speaker, charging port, and
battery under normal consumer use.

The warranty does not cover accidental damage, liquid damage, cosmetic wear,
loss, or damage caused by an unapproved charger. A customer must provide an
order number or another proof of purchase before the support team can open a
warranty assessment.

Support agents must not promise a replacement before the diagnostic assessment
is complete. If the item is confirmed defective within 30 days of delivery,
escalate the case as `CS-DEFECT-30D` for priority review.
